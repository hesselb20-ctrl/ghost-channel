Photosensitivity warning: debug mode makes the picture flicker on purpose at the carrier frequency (20 Hz on a 60 Hz screen). That is inside the 3–30 Hz range that can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy. Amplitude stays capped at 9 % so no flash reaches the 10 % relative-luminance threshold in WCAG 2.3.1, and neighbouring quadrants flicker in opposite directions. Still: do not use it in front of an audience.
